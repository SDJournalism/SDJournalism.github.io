Liverpool vs Manchester City Preview: Maresca's Perfect Record Meets Iraola's Anfield Wall

Manchester City travel to Anfield on Sunday as the Premier League's only perfect side, five wins from five under Enzo Maresca, to face a Liverpool team that has not lost in seven matches in all competitions since Andoni Iraola took charge (The Stats Zone, 2026).

City's flawless start under Maresca

Maresca's side opened up a three-point lead at the top before the international break by beating Sunderland 5-3 at the Etihad, a chaotic afternoon in which City took the lead three times in the first half and Brian Brobbey still scored a hat-trick for the visitors (Premier League, 2026). Maresca did not pretend it was a perfect performance: "There were too many goals. I prefer 1-0, it's much better!" he said (Premier League, 2026).

City have now scored in 21 consecutive Premier League matches, eight short of the club record, and are unbeaten in their last ten away games stretching back to the start of February (The Stats Zone, 2026).

Iraola builds Liverpool from the back

Liverpool's league form has been less spectacular, with three draws in their first five matches leaving them sixth on nine points, but Iraola's side are hard to break down. Alexander Isak's second-half goal earned a 1-0 win at Bournemouth on Iraola's return to his old club (Premier League, 2026), and it was Liverpool's third consecutive league clean sheet, the joint-most of any side this season (The Stats Zone, 2026).

Injury worries in the Liverpool attack

The biggest question for Iraola is up front. Isak, who has four goals this season, left Sweden's camp early after a heavy stamp on his toe during a 2-1 Nations League win over Romania, and called the setback "a real blow" (Goal, 2026). Liverpool have not set a return date, though the two-week gap before Sunday gives him a chance to be ready (Yahoo Sports, 2026).

Cody Gakpo is set to miss the game after damaging his ankle in a tackle from Sasa Lukic while playing for the Netherlands against Serbia, with Dutch reports suggesting he will be out for several weeks (Empire of the Kop, 2026). Florian Wirtz is a doubt, while Giovanni Leoni, Conor Bradley and Hugo Ekitike remain unavailable (The Stats Zone, 2026).

Foden's ban bites for City

City will be without Phil Foden, who serves the last game of a three-match domestic ban for violent conduct after he was sent off 23 minutes into the Manchester derby for his reaction to Bruno Fernandes (Read Man City, 2026). Antoine Semenyo is injured and Nico O'Reilly is a doubt, but Jeremy Doku is fit again (The Stats Zone, 2026).

The verdict hanging over City

City also arrive at Anfield under the shadow of an independent commission's verdict, which found the club guilty of all of the serious breaches of Premier League financial rules between 2009-10 and 2017-18 (Al Jazeera, 2026). Sanctions have not yet been decided, and City have said they will appeal, insisting that "the club is innocent of the accusations made by the Premier League" (Al Jazeera, 2026).

Recent history favours City

City won both league meetings last season, 3-0 at the Etihad and 2-1 at Anfield (The Stats Zone, 2026), before knocking Liverpool out of the FA Cup with a 4-0 quarter-final win at the Etihad in April, Erling Haaland scoring a hat-trick (Manchester City, 2026). Liverpool's overall Premier League record against City at Anfield is still strong, though: 18 wins, eight draws and only three defeats (The Stats Zone, 2026).

Sunday's game is the first meeting between Iraola and Maresca since both men took over two of the league's biggest clubs this summer. City's perfect start will come up against three straight Liverpool clean sheets, and if Liverpool keep a fourth, City's 21-game scoring run will end at Anfield.
