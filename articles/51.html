Manchester United 4-4 Aston Villa

Manchester United 4-4 Aston Villa: Dorgu's 96th-Minute Header Caps Old Trafford Chaos

Manchester United came from two goals down twice to draw 4-4 with Aston Villa at Old Trafford, scoring twice in stoppage time through substitutes Joshua Zirkzee and Patrick Dorgu, but the point still left them in the relegation zone (Sky Sports, 2026).

Villa two up inside four minutes

Villa could not have started faster. Alysson scored inside the first minute, nutmegging Senne Lammens with a first-time finish from John McGinn's pass for his first goal for the club (Aston Villa, 2026). Three minutes later Lammens spilled another Alysson shot and Nicolas Jackson finished from close range (Sky Sports, 2026), his ninth goal of the season (Aston Villa, 2026). United had gone two down in a club-record 3 minutes 42 seconds (ESPN, 2026).

United fight back before the break

United's way back came from the penalty spot. Boubacar Kamara fouled Matheus Cunha in the box, and Bruno Fernandes sent Zion Suzuki the wrong way in the 16th minute (Aston Villa, 2026). Suzuki kept Villa in front with a fine save from a curling Cunha shot, but Youri Tielemans steered in Noussair Mazraoui's cut-back on 44 minutes to make it 2-2 at half-time (Aston Villa, 2026), scoring against his former club (ESPN, 2026).

Villa strike again after half-time

The second half started just like the first. Diogo Dalot brought down Alysson and McGinn converted the penalty in the 48th minute, and five minutes later McGinn's free-kick hit the crossbar off Leny Yoro's head, Jackson reached the rebound first and Leon Goretzka scored from close range for his first Villa goal (Aston Villa, 2026). At 4-2, Villa looked set to pile even more pressure on Carrick (ESPN, 2026).

Carrick sent on Shea Lacey, Mason Mount and Dorgu for Amad, Tielemans and Dalot after 66 minutes (Premier League News Now, 2026), and Dalot's substitution was loudly cheered by the home crowd (ESPN, 2026). United kept pushing: Suzuki saved from Amad, Bryan Mbeumo drove a shot into the side-netting and Mount hit the crossbar late on (Aston Villa, 2026).

Two goals in stoppage time

Villa were still 4-2 up going into second-half stoppage time (Aston Villa, 2026). Zirkzee swept home in the 92nd minute to give United hope (Sky Sports, 2026), and in the 96th minute Dorgu rose to head in a Fernandes corner. Suzuki appealed that he had been impeded going for the cross, but the goal stood (ESPN, 2026).

What the managers said

Carrick was frustrated by the way Villa's goals came about: "We kind of beat ourselves a little bit with the moments really," he said, pointing to "totally avoidable goals that we're giving away" (Sky Sports, 2026). He added: "We haven't won enough games, but I expect to move forward and keep working what we've been working on, tighten up on a few mistakes, and win football matches" (ESPN, 2026).

Unai Emery, in charge for his 200th Premier League game (Aston Villa, 2026), did not hide his feelings: "Angry, of course. To accept to lose in the last minutes two goals, it is difficult," he said. "I am angry for the situation, not with the players" (Sky Sports, 2026).

"I am angry for the situation, not with the players."

Fight, but still in the bottom three

Roy Keane gave United some credit on Sky Sports: "They kept going, so you have to give them a little bit of praise" (Sky Sports, 2026). Supporters chanted Carrick's name at the final whistle, while a banner reading "we don't want a new stadium, we want new owners" was aimed at the Glazers and Sir Jim Ratcliffe (ESPN, 2026).

The draw stretched United's run without a league win to eight games and left them 18th going into the international break (ESPN, 2026), while Villa, now six games unbeaten, moved up to 10th (Aston Villa, 2026). For Carrick, there would be no next game: United sacked him four days later (The Mag, 2026).
