Cody Gakpo Closing In On £60m Tottenham Move as Liverpool Eye Barcola

Tottenham's remarkable summer of recruitment is set to add another name. Cody Gakpo is closing in on a move to north London, with Liverpool and Spurs said to have agreed a fee in principle of around £60m for the Dutch forward (FootballTransfers, 2026).

Personal terms already agreed

Gakpo has reportedly agreed personal terms on a five-year contract at Tottenham, with the move now largely a matter of the two clubs finalising the structure of the fee. It would represent one of the more eye-catching additions yet to Roberto De Zerbi's rebuilt squad (This Is Anfield, 2026).

Fitting the De Zerbi rebuild

Gakpo would join a Tottenham forward line already reshaped this summer around Sandro Tonali, Mateus Fernandes and Jan Paul van Hecke, with De Zerbi building a squad capable of competing on multiple fronts under a clearer attacking identity. A versatile forward comfortable across the front line, capable of playing centrally or from the left, adds further depth to that project.

Liverpool's reinvestment plan

For Liverpool, the sale would free up significant funds to pursue a marquee attacking addition of their own. Andoni Iraola's side are understood to be eyeing a move for Barcelona's Bradley Barcola, viewed at Anfield as a long-term successor in the wide forward positions, with the Gakpo fee earmarked to help finance a bid (FourFourTwo, 2026).

A fee still to be finalised

Both clubs are understood to be working through the final structure of payments before any deal is confirmed, and there remains a chance the transfer could yet stall over the finer details -- but sources close to the negotiations describe it as advanced and increasingly likely to be completed before the window closes (FootballTransfers, 2026).

It's a deal that, if completed, continues a trend that has defined Tottenham's summer: use financial firepower and a clear project under De Zerbi to convince players from directly rival Premier League clubs to make the move -- a pattern already seen with Cristian Romero's exit from the club in the opposite direction, and now potentially repeated in reverse with Gakpo.
