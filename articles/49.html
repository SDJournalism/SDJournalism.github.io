Manchester United vs Tottenham Preview: Carrick and De Zerbi Meet With the Pressure Rising

Saturday evening's game at Old Trafford brings together two managers under real pressure: Michael Carrick's Manchester United are 12th with one league win from five, while Roberto De Zerbi's Tottenham are bottom of the table with just two points (The Stats Zone, 2026).

United searching for a response

United's five points equal their lowest total after five Premier League games, and they went into the international break ten points behind leaders Manchester City after a 1-1 draw at Fulham (Al Jazeera, 2026). Carrick stayed upbeat afterwards: "I'm not despondent because I think we've shown some good signs as well," he said. "I'm convinced we'll have a good season" (Al Jazeera, 2026).

Their only league win so far was a 5-2 thrashing of Ipswich Town, with defeats to Hull City and Manchester City and draws against Everton and Fulham making up the rest (Greenball360, 2026). United have also conceded in all five of their league matches (The Stats Zone, 2026).

Spurs bottom and struggling for goals

Tottenham's problems are at the other end of the pitch. They have failed to score in four of their five league games, and their two points equal the club's lowest total at this stage of a Premier League season (The Stats Zone, 2026). Both of their league goals came late in a 3-2 home defeat to Aston Villa, despite a summer spend of more than £300m (Football365, 2026).

De Zerbi said his side had been "too fragile" against Villa and took the blame himself: "If we didn't score it's my responsibility... We're going to work harder, but football is like life. You are strong or you lose" (101 Great Goals, 2026).

"Football is like life. You are strong or you lose."

Team news at Old Trafford

Carlos Baleba, United's £70m summer signing from Brighton, played in a behind-closed-doors friendly against Lausanne-Sport during the break and could make his competitive debut (Sports Mole, 2026). Matthijs de Ligt, Manuel Ugarte and Amad remain sidelined, but Harry Maguire and Lisandro Martinez have recovered (Greenball360, 2026).

Bruno Fernandes is a worry. Portugal boss Jorge Jesus said the United captain "played with an injury he brought from Manchester United" before he withdrew from their game against Norway, while Patrick Dorgu lasted only half an hour of Denmark's win over Wales with a muscle problem (K24 Digital, 2026).

Tottenham will be without Xavi Simons, Dejan Kulusevski, Mykhailo Mudryk and Wilson Odobert, and Pedro Porro picked up a hamstring problem in the defeat to Villa (Greenball360, 2026).

A game neither manager can lose

History is on United's side at Old Trafford, where they have won 25 of their 34 Premier League meetings with Tottenham (The Stats Zone, 2026). Recent results point the other way, though: Spurs have won five of the last ten competitive meetings between the clubs, United only two (Greenball360, 2026).

Sky Sports pundit Alan Smith summed up what is at stake for Carrick: "I'm not saying he would get the sack, but there'd be so many questions levelled at him if they were to lose to Spurs at Old Trafford" (Read Man Utd, 2026). With Tottenham still looking for a first league win, De Zerbi can't afford a defeat either.
