Mauricio Pochettino at Manchester United: The Dream Job Arrives at the Hardest Moment

Manchester United have finally appointed the manager they first wanted in 2018. The club confirmed on Friday that Mauricio Pochettino "has agreed to become the Manchester United men's head coach from 18 November" (Goal, 2026), one day after Michael Carrick was sacked with United 18th in the Premier League (ABC News, 2026). Eight years ago, when Jose Mourinho was sacked, Pochettino was reported to be United's preferred candidate to take over (Sports Illustrated, 2018).

The deal

The 54-year-old has signed a two-and-a-half-year contract (ABC News, 2026) running until the summer of 2029, and Sky Sports News understands United will pay around £7.4m to buy him out of his USA contract (Sky Sports, 2026). He was the only coach United approached directly, the "unanimous choice recommended by the club's hierarchy" (Sky Sports, 2026).

The club's statement described him as "renowned for his high-intensity, attacking style of football, his dedication to building strong, cohesive teams and his outstanding record of developing individual players" (CNN, 2026). He becomes the eighth permanent manager at Old Trafford since Sir Alex Ferguson retired in 2013 (Al Jazeera, 2026).

A tearful goodbye to the USA

Pochettino will not start straight away. He stays in charge of the USA for their two-legged Concacaf Nations League quarter-final against Haiti on 14 and 17 November (US Soccer, 2026). He told his players on Thursday, and was in tears as he explained his decision at a news conference at the team's training camp (ABC News, 2026).

"They know that there's not too many opportunities (that) can happen in your career like a side of Manchester United and, of course, when that arrives, it's difficult to turn down," he said (ABC News, 2026). In a statement released through US Soccer, he added: "The opportunity to represent the USA crest was one of the greatest honours of my career" (Al Jazeera, 2026).

"When that arrives, it's difficult to turn down."

He leaves having led the co-hosts to the round of 16 at this summer's World Cup, with a record of 21 wins, 12 defeats and two draws going into the Haiti games (ABC News, 2026). Only three months ago, US Soccer had announced a contract to keep him in charge until the 2030 World Cup (ABC News, 2026).

The record he brings

Pochettino has managed Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea in the Premier League, as well as Espanyol in Spain (ABC News, 2026). At Spurs he reached a League Cup final and the 2019 Champions League final, and at Paris Saint-Germain he won Ligue 1 and the French Cup in a season and a half (Sky Sports, 2026). The gap on his CV is a major trophy in England: he lost the 2024 League Cup final with Chelsea (ABC News, 2026).

What waits at Old Trafford

There is no time to settle in. His first game is away at Liverpool on Sunday 22 November, followed by Sporting in the Champions League, Brentford at home, a trip to Newcastle, Coventry at home and RB Leipzig (Sky Sports, 2026).

He also inherits a squad assembled at considerable expense but short of confidence and identity, with fans booing the players off after the home defeat to Bournemouth (Al Jazeera, 2026). Gary Neville says Pochettino has to take control of recruitment, while Jamie Redknapp believes his first job is to get the squad fit and improve the back four (Sky Sports, 2026).

The Manchester United Supporters' Trust welcomed him but put the onus on the owners: "We now hope the club backs the new manager and gives him all the power, resources and tools he needs to succeed" (Sky Sports, 2026). United have paid more than $100m in severance to coaches and managers over the past 13 years (ABC News, 2026). Pochettino has the CV to turn this around, but he will need time and backing that the club has rarely given its managers since Ferguson left.
