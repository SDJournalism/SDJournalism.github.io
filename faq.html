Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers before you email me directly.

Who writes everything on this site?

Every article and Tactical Lab piece is written and researched by me, Samuel Davies -- a sport student based in Manchester with a focus on tactics, players and the stories behind the scoreline.

What's the difference between Articles and Tactical Lab?

Articles cover match reports, previews, transfer news and opinion. Tactical Lab is the visual side of the site -- moving pitch diagrams that break down a manager's system or a player's signature movement pattern, grouped into Manager DNA, Player Blueprints and the Tactical Vault.

Can I use or republish your work?

Feel free to share a link to any article or breakdown. Please don't copy or republish full pieces, quotes or diagrams elsewhere without asking first -- email me and I'm happy to talk about it.

How do I suggest a topic, team or player breakdown?

Email me your idea. I'm always looking for suggestions, and reader requests regularly turn into full pieces.

Are you available for freelance work?

Yes -- I take on match reports, previews, analysis and opinion pieces for commission or ongoing contributor work. Head to the About page for more, or get in touch directly.

Where do your sources and stats come from?

Every article and Tactical Lab breakdown links its sources at the bottom of the piece, so you can check the reporting for yourself.
