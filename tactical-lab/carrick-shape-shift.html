The Shape-Shift

Manchester United finished 15th last season. Since Michael Carrick took interim charge in January 2026, no side in the Premier League has taken more points -- and the turnaround has been built on a formation that quietly changes shape depending on whether United have the ball.

A back four that isn't always a back four

Carrick inherited Ruben Amorim's back three and moved away from it within weeks, returning to an orthodox 4-2-3-1. But the shape doesn't stay a back four once United have the ball: one full-back tucks inside or drops deep to form a temporary back three, while the wingers hold their width high up the pitch. It converts the team into a 3-2-5 in attack without United ever looking like they've changed formation at all (Total Football Analysis, 2026).

Repositioning Bruno Fernandes

The clearest individual change has been Bruno Fernandes, pulled out of the deeper central role Amorim had used him in and pushed back between the lines, screening Casemiro and Kobbie Mainoo's double pivot and directing play out to the wings. Matheus Cunha tucks infield from the left early to add a fourth central body, turning what used to be a flat midfield into a fluid, overlapping attacking line (Total Football Analysis, 2026).

The numbers behind the run

The form has been extraordinary by any measure: 12 wins from 17 Premier League matches in interim charge, worth 2.29 points per game -- a pace that would be worth 87 points across a full season, and more points than champions Arsenal collected over the same run of fixtures. Wins over Manchester City, Arsenal, Liverpool and Chelsea followed, along with Champions League qualification after a two-year absence (Planet Football, 2026).

What the dressing room says

Harry Maguire has pointed to an early turning point: "We managed to get six points and from then on everyone has believed in it and we've got confidence" (Al Jazeera, 2026). Bruno Fernandes has been just as direct about Carrick's own game intelligence: "When, as a player, you can see and think about the game like him, you can also do it from the bench... He's done a fantastic job" (Al Jazeera, 2026). United made the appointment permanent on 22 May 2026. The next test is whether an interim-manager bounce can survive a full pre-season and a first complete campaign in charge.
